Star-Delta (Y-Δ) Motor Starter — PLC + Push-button + HMI

Press START / STOP on the operator panel or the HMI screen and watch each step.

① Ready

Supply is on. KM1, KM2, KM3 are all OFF. PLC waits for START from the push-button or HMI.

② Star start (Y)

PLC energizes KM1 + KM3. Windings get 230 V, starting current is only about ⅓. Timer T1 runs for 5 s.

③ Changeover

T1 done: PLC drops KM3 first, then waits 0.5 s interlock so KM3 and KM2 are never closed together (no short circuit).

④ Delta run (Δ)

PLC energizes KM2. Windings get full 400 V → full torque and rated speed. KM1 + KM2 stay on.

STOP (push-button or HMI) from any step: PLC de-energizes all contactors and the motor coasts to a stop.

🏭 Real industrial example: Air-compressor house, textile factory

Screw compressor motor: 37 kW, 400 V, 50 Hz, In ≈ 68 A, 1450 rpm. Direct-on-line start draws about 7 × In; star-delta cuts that to one third, so the factory voltage does not dip and other machines keep running.

  • MCCB 80 A – short-circuit protection
  • KM1, KM2: 40 A (AC-3) – carry 0.58 × In = 39 A
  • KM3 star: 25 A (AC-3) – carries 0.33 × In = 22 A
  • Overload relay 39 A – set to 0.58 × In (it sits in the delta line)
  • Star time 5–10 s – compressor starts unloaded (star torque is only ⅓)
  • Same method: pumps, fans, conveyors, crushers, mixers
